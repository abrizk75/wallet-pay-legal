Privacy Policy

Wallet Pay — Global Wallet Payments for Shopify

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Wallet Pay ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you install and use the Wallet Pay application (the "App") on your Shopify store.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Merchant Information

  • Shop domain (e.g., yourstore.myshopify.com)
  • Email address (for notifications)
  • Wallet account details you configure (wallet numbers, handles, etc.)
  • App preferences and settings

2.2 Customer Information

When a customer places an order using a wallet payment method, we collect:

  • Customer name
  • Customer email address
  • Order number and amount
  • Payment method chosen
  • Payment proof uploaded by the customer (screenshot)
  • Transaction reference number (if provided)
  • Sender phone number (if provided)

3. How We Use Information

  • Process and track wallet payments
  • Send transactional emails to merchants and customers
  • Provide the merchant dashboard and reporting
  • Verify payment proofs and prevent fraudulent submissions
  • Improve the App and provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We use unique tokens for each payment to prevent duplicate submissions.

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality. When you uninstall the App, all your data is permanently deleted within 48 hours.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Shopify — for platform integration (see Shopify's Privacy Policy)
  • Resend — for sending transactional emails
  • Cloud hosting providers — for secure data storage

6. Customer Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Customers whose data we process have the right to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of their data
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at ab.rizk1975@gmail.com.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only:

  • With Shopify (as required by the platform)
  • With service providers who help us operate the App
  • When required by law

8. Cookies

The App uses minimal cookies required for authentication and functionality. No tracking cookies are used for advertising purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or via email.
